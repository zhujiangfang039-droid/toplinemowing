Pricing depends on lawn size, access, grass condition, service frequency, and any extra trimming needed. Contact us for a free estimate tailored to your property.

Yes. TOPLINE MOWING serves residential customers and commercial properties within our service area.

How far do you travel?+

We generally serve properties within about 30 miles of our home base near Cypress and Northwest Houston. Call us to confirm your address.

Can I request recurring mowing?+

Yes. Tell us the schedule you have in mind and we'll recommend a plan based on your property and seasonal growth.

What is included with mowing?+

A typical visit includes lawn mowing, trimming around obstacles and borders, edging accessible hard surfaces, and blowing clippings from sidewalks and driveways. Your estimate will confirm the exact scope.
